Getting started

Cricket.lk is a web app you install on your phone. It works without an account and without internet — everything you score is saved on your device.

Install it on your phone Beginner

Open the app's address in your phone's browser. A banner at the bottom shows how to install it for your browser.

  • Android (Chrome): tap Install on the banner, or ⋮ → Install app.
  • iPhone (Safari): tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • In WhatsApp / Instagram's built-in browser, open the link in Chrome or Safari first.

After that, launch Cricket.lk from your home screen like any other app. It opens with a short splash screen while it loads.

Good to knowInstalled, it works fully offline — handy at grounds with no signal.

Find your way around Beginner

Home is your starting point: New match (the big green tile), New tournament, Teams, Players, Presets and Stats.

The ☰ menu (top left) reaches every screen, including Premier, Settings and Sign in. The top-right buttons are sign-in/your account and sound on/off.

Acting as on Home switches between scoring as a Guest (on this device) and as one of your clubs — see chapter 6.

TipYour phone's back button works everywhere: it closes sheets first, then goes back a screen.
2

Score your first match

A full match, ball by ball: set it up, pick the openers, score runs, extras and wickets, and finish with a result.

1 · Set up the match Beginner

  1. On Home, tap New match.
  2. Pick a preset — T20, T10 or Tape-ball. It fills in the playing conditions. Tap the i next to a preset to read exactly what it means.
  3. Type the two team names.
  4. Change anything you like: balls per over, overs, players per side, last man stands, what a wide or no-ball is worth, free hits, and whether a catch/run-out needs the fielder's name. Leave overs or players blank for no limit.
  5. Choose who bats first and tap Start match.
TipType the name of a saved team (chapter 4) and only its players are suggested for that side.

2 · Name the openers and the first bowler Beginner

Type (or pick from the list) the striker, non-striker and the bowler, then tap Start innings. Names you've used before are suggested as you type.

Got the toss wrong? Tap Wrong toss? to swap who bats first.

Rule built inA player can't play for both teams in one match — the app stops you if you type a name that's already on the other side.

3 · The live scoring screen Beginner

Everything fits on one screen:

  • Score, overs and run rate at the top (and the target, runs needed and required rate in a chase).
  • The batters (▶ marks the striker), the bowler, the partnership and last wicket.
  • The over rail — every ball of this over, and the last one.
  • The run pad: tap 0–6 after each ball. Strike changes and overs end automatically.
  • UNDO takes back the last ball — use it freely.
TipThe ⋯ button at the top opens Match options (step 7).

4 · Extras: wides, no-balls, byes, leg byes Beginner

Tap Wide, No ball, Bye or Leg bye. A sheet asks how many runs were run or hit on top of the extra — tap 0 for just the wide or no-ball, 4 if it went for four, and so on. Each tile also shows what the ball is worth in total, e.g. a wide that went for four is Wd+4 · 5 total.

  • Wides and no-balls don't count as a ball of the over; byes and leg byes do.
  • Byes and leg byes go to the team, not against the bowler.
  • A no-ball gives a free hit (if your preset says so) — only a run out is possible on it.

5 · Wickets Beginner

Tap WICKET and choose how they got out.

  • Bowled, LBW, Stumped, Hit wicket — one tap and it's done.
  • Caught — add the fielder's name (credits the catch), then Confirm wicket.
  • Run out — pick who's out (striker or non-striker), at which end, and the runs completed before it, then confirm.
TipThe sheets show the current score in a banner at the top, so you never lose track.

6 · New batter, new bowler Beginner

After a wicket the app asks for the new batter (it shows how the last one got out). At the end of each over it asks for the next bowler:

  • Anyone who has already bowled is one tap away, with their figures.
  • Last over's bowler is crossed out — no one bowls two overs in a row.

Need a moment? Tap Pick later — or drag the sheet down — to look at the scorecard or menu first. A + Pick batsman chip waits in the empty slot, and tapping any run button brings the sheet back.

7 · Match options & the full scorecard Intermediate

The ⋯ button opens Match options:

  • Change who bats first — restarts the same match from ball one (Undo brings it back).
  • End innings — rain, time, a declaration.
  • Forfeit — one team concedes; pick the team, then confirm.
  • Cancel match — discard it with no result.
  • Switch players (when the sides come from saved teams) and Show nicknames (club matches).

Full scorecard under the Wicket button opens every innings, with a Ball by ball tab listing each over.

8 · Innings break and the result Beginner

When the first innings ends you get the innings break: top scorers, best bowlers and the target. Tap Start 2nd innings and name the new openers.

When the chase ends, the Result screen shows who won and by how much, and the Player of the match — picked automatically from runs, wickets and catches. You can change it.

The match is saved to your history: it feeds Stats, MVP and any points table.

9 · Quick scoring without names Beginner

No time for names? Tick Score without player names at the bottom of match setup. You go straight to the run pad and never get asked for a name.

  • Batters appear as Batter 1, Batter 2…, and each over's bowler as Over 1, Over 2…
  • Team scores, results and points tables work as normal; these matches just stay out of player stats.

Share & export

Send a scorecard to the team group, or keep a PDF.

Share a scorecard image Beginner

  1. On the Result screen tap Share image.
  2. Pick the look: Night or Day.
  3. Tap Share to send it to WhatsApp, Instagram and so on, or Save image.

The image has both innings, every batter and bowler, the player of the match, and the Cricket.lk logo.

Any finished matchNot just right after the match: open any completed match — from Home's Recent matches, a tournament's fixtures or a club's match list — and its scorecard sheet has Share image and Export PDF too.

Export a PDF Beginner

Tap Export PDF, then choose Save as PDF in your phone's print screen. You get a clean, printable scorecard — the full version of every innings — with the Cricket.lk header, named after the match.

Also for tournamentsAn ended tournament's Summary has its own Share and Export PDF (chapter 5).

Teams, players & presets

Save your regular sides and playing conditions so setting up a match takes seconds. All of this stays on your device.

Teams and players Intermediate

Teams: add a team, then add its players. Players: everyone you've ever named, with their team.

When you type a saved team's name at match setup, its players become that side's squad: only they're suggested while scoring, and you can move players between sides for one match from ⋯ → Switch players.

Presets Intermediate

A match preset is a set of playing conditions (overs, balls per over, players, wides, no-balls, free hit, fielder rules). A tournament preset adds points for a win/tie/loss and whether net run rate is used.

The built-in T20, T10 and Tape-ball presets can be edited or deleted like your own. Tap + New match preset for your Sunday rules.

Stats and MVP Intermediate

Stats leads with the MVP table (points for runs, boundaries, wickets, maidens, catches and run-outs), then Batting, Bowling and Fielding tabs. It reads every completed match on your device.

5

Tournaments

Run a league: teams play fixtures, the points table and net run rate update themselves, and at the end you get a shareable summary.

Create a tournament Intermediate

  1. Home → New tournament.
  2. Name it, pick a tournament preset (points and NRR) and tick the teams taking part.
  3. Tap Create tournament.

The dashboard has four tabs: Overview (points table and latest results), Teams (tap a team to see its players), Fixtures and Stats.

Play fixtures — or just enter the result Intermediate

On Fixtures, pick two teams and who bats first, then Start match to score it ball by ball.

Scored somewhere else, or only know the totals? Tap Enter result only and fill in each team's runs and wickets (overs are optional). It counts in the points table straight away; it just has no scorecard or player stats.

Net run rateIf you leave overs blank, that innings counts as the full overs quota — the same rule as an all-out innings.

End the tournament and share the results Intermediate

  1. On Overview tap 🏁 End tournament and confirm.
  2. A new Summary tab appears: the champions, runner-up, totals, MVP, most runs and wickets, best innings and bowling, the highest total and the final table.
  3. Tap Share results for a poster-style image (Night or Day), or Export PDF.

Changed your mind? Reopen tournament at the bottom of Summary.

Accounts & clubs

Sign in to run a club in the cloud: a shared roster, presets and matches that every club admin sees on their own phone.

Sign up, then create or join a club Advanced

  1. Tap Sign in (top right) → Sign up, and confirm your email.
  2. Open your account (👤) → My clubs → create a club.
  3. On Home, set Acting as to your club. The tiles change to the club's own Practice match (club vs itself), Friendly match (vs visitors), New tournament, Players, Presets and Stats.
Kept apartGuest data (on your device) and club data (in the cloud) never mix. Switch back to Guest any time.

Club roster and player profiles Advanced

Club Players is the roster. Tap + to add someone: name, plus optional contact number, NIC, nickname, jersey number, city, batting/bowling style, keeper and a photo. If they're already on the platform, the sheet suggests them so you don't create a duplicate.

Tap a player for their profile: details, and a Stats tab with separate Club and Premier batting, bowling and fielding records.

PrivacyContact number and NIC are only visible to people who may manage that profile.

Club home, admins and presets Advanced

Account → My clubs → your club shows its tournaments, admins and matches. Invite an admin by email — if they already have an account they're added at once; otherwise they become an admin as soon as they sign up and confirm that email.

Club Presets work like your own, but are shared by everyone in the club.

NicknamesIn a club match, typing a nickname or #7 finds the right player, and ⋯ → Show nicknames shows them on the scoreboard.
7

Club tournaments & teams

The classic club day: split the club into teams, play each other, crown a winner.

Build the teams Advanced

  1. Home (acting as your club) → New tournament.
  2. On its Teams tab, tap Split the roster into 2 random teams, or + Add team and pick players yourself.
  3. In the team sheet, tap a player to add them, tap × to take them off, or + All free for everyone not on a team.

Each player can be on one team per tournament.

New members, guests and moving players Advanced

Type a new name in the search box and tap + Add to this team…. You're asked who they are:

  • New club player — joins the club roster for good.
  • Guest player — plays for this team only; nothing about them is stored in the club.

Tapping someone who is already on another team asks "Move …?" first, so a stray tap can't reshuffle the teams.

Play the matches Advanced

On the tournament's Overview or Fixtures tap Start match: pick the two teams from tiles instead of typing names. Only their players are suggested while scoring. Enter result only and End tournament work exactly as in chapter 5.

8

Offline & sync

No signal at the ground? Keep going. Club matches, players, teams and presets are saved on your phone and sent to the cloud when you're back online.

The sync pill Advanced

When something is waiting, a small pill appears in the top bar: Offline, 3 to sync, Syncing… or 1 not synced. Tap it to see every change in the queue.

  • Everything syncs by itself when the connection returns.
  • If the server refuses a change (say, a team name that's taken), it waits for you with Retry and Discard.
  • Items not yet synced show a not synced tag.
Needs internetSigning in, inviting admins, contact/NIC details, claims and Premier need a connection.

Premier, settings & tips

The cross-club player list, making a profile yours, and making the app look the way you like.

Premier and claiming your profile Advanced

Premier (☰ menu) lists every player on the platform, from every club, with teams and stats across clubs.

Found yourself? Open the profile and tap This is me — claim profile. A platform admin approves it; then you manage your own details. If there are two profiles of you, ask to merge them — your stats from both are kept.

Settings Beginner

☰ → Settings: turn the scoring sounds on or off, and pick a theme — Midnight, Slate, Daylight or Sunset.

Tips & questions

I tapped the wrong button while scoring.

Tap UNDO — as many times as you need. It also undoes wickets, extras and the end of an over.

Can I leave the scoring screen during a match?

Yes. Use the ☰ menu; the match keeps running. Get back with the green "Live" pill at the top, or "Resume live match" on Home. Closing or reloading the app always brings you back to the match.

How do I close a sheet?

Drag it down from the top, tap ✕ / Cancel, or press your phone's back button.

A batter or bowler has to leave for a moment.

Tap "Pick later" on the sheet (or drag it down). The empty slot shows "+ Pick…", and scoring waits until you fill it.

There's no overs limit — how does the innings end?

All out, the target reached, or "End innings" under the Wicket button. Any match can also be ended early from ⋯ → End innings.

Where is my data?

Guest matches, teams and players are stored on this phone only. Club data is in the cloud, visible to that club's admins. Clearing your browser data removes guest data.
