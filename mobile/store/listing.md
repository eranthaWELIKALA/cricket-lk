## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Cricket.lk is a ball-by-ball cricket scorer for club games, tape-ball
matches, school cricket and weekend tournaments. Tap a button for each ball
and the scorecard, run rate, partnerships and target keep themselves up to
date.

SCORE EVERY BALL
• Runs, fours and sixes in one tap, plus wides, no-balls, byes and leg byes
• Every way out: bowled, caught, LBW, run out (at either end), stumped, hit wicket, including wickets off a wide or no-ball
• Free hits, retired hurt, last man stands
• Undo, or go back and fix, add or remove any earlier ball
• Fell behind? Catch up the score, or just type in the final totals

YOUR OWN RULES
• Balls per over, overs, players per side, wide and no-ball runs
• No overs limit or no wickets limit if you like
• Save your conditions as presets: T20, T10, tape-ball, or your own
• Ties can go to a super over

TOURNAMENTS
• Teams, fixtures and a points table with net run rate
• Enter a result without scoring it ball by ball
• End the tournament for a summary: champion, top run scorer, top wicket taker, best figures

STATS
• Batting, bowling and fielding leaderboards and records
• Player of the match and MVP points, with adjustable points systems
• Full scorecards, ball by ball and commentary for every match

SHARE
• Share a scorecard image to WhatsApp or anywhere else
• Save it to your gallery, or export a PDF

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up, no ads
• Works fully offline: the app has no internet access at all
• Everything you enter stays on your phone

## Data safety (Google Play) / privacy questions (Galaxy Store)

- Does the app collect or share any user data? **No.** The app has no
  internet permission, no analytics, no ads and no account. What people
  enter stays on the device.
- Data that Android's own backup copies is not collection by the developer.
  The app allows Android backup, so a user's matches move to a new phone.
- Permissions: **none**.

## Other Play Console answers

- **App access:** all functionality is available without logging in.
- **Ads:** no ads.
- **Content rating questionnaire:** not a game; no violence, sexual content,
  bad language, drugs, gambling or purchases. There's no user-to-user
  interaction: sharing only hands an image to another app the user chooses,
  through Android's share sheet. Expected rating: Everyone / PEGI 3.
- **Target audience:** 13 and over keeps the app outside the Families policy.
  Choosing younger ages too is allowed, but adds Families requirements.
- **News app / government app / financial features / health:** no.
