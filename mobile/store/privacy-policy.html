Cricket.lk privacy policy

Cricket.lk for Android, by Erantha Welikala · Effective 8 November 2026

Cricket.lk is a cricket scoring app. This policy covers the Android app available on Google Play and the Samsung Galaxy Store.

We collect nothing

The app has no account, no sign-up, no advertising and no analytics. It also has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything from your phone to us or to anyone else. We don't see your matches, names or anything else you enter.

What stays on your phone

The matches, teams, player names, tournaments, presets and settings you enter are saved on your phone only, so you can come back to them. You can delete them at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this data in your device backup, so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by your device and your backup provider under your own settings, not by us.

When you share

Nothing leaves the app unless you ask. If you tap Share, the scorecard image goes to the app you pick from Android's share sheet (for example a messaging app), under that app's own privacy policy. Save image saves it to your phone's Pictures folder. Export PDF opens Android's print dialog, where you choose to save or print it.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
